    Missable warning — read first. Only one trophy is possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot). After the Chapter 1 mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen at Jason’s house in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. That meditation vanishes after Chapter 1, and it is still unconfirmed whether it is required — PowerPyx’s author did it and then earned the trophy, so do it to be safe. Everything else can be done after the story in free-roam. Sources: PowerPyx, GTABase, KeenGamer.
